Welcome to Loula Pridham's Portfolio

My name is Loula Pridham and I am a first year Digital Media Ba student at the University of Leeds. I am 18 years old and am from Leigh-on-sea in Essex. I have a strong passion for graphic design, photography and editing and have been recently introduced to interface design through my studies.

This portolio showcases a range of web design protects I have created throughout my course workshops this term. These projects demonstrate my skills in HTML, CSS and website design.
